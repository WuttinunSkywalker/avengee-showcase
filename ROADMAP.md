## Showcase 6: Klin (3D perfume house, `/[lang]/klin`)

Asked for on 2026-11-01: a showcase that goes big on visual effects, 3D and scroll-linked animation. Research: award-winning sites mostly use three.js (often through React Three Fiber) for WebGL, GSAP + ScrollTrigger for timelines (free since 2025) and Lenis for weighted scrolling; Unity WebGL exports are 10–50 MB and load slowly, which suits games, not websites.

- [x] First release: a procedural glass flacon (lathe geometry, physical glass and juice, brass collar) in a fixed WebGL layer behind the page. Scroll drives one continuous scene: the bottle turns one way, its juice and the page colour change per scent, rain → jasmine petals → rising embers, then the camera pulls back to a line-up of all three. Lenis smooth scrolling, text rising from masks, a pop-up scent sheet (drawn bottle that fills, sizes, add to bag with a popping count), bag popover, Thai and English, reduced motion keeps everything readable and still
- [x] Found while building it: chapter text is pinned with `position: sticky`, so the scent handover is measured from the layout (hold while pinned, blend between one panel letting go and the next pinning) rather than from the middle of the screen; otherwise phones showed one scent's text over the next scent's colours
- [x] Found while building it: the React compiler lint rules reject mutating memoised three.js objects inside `useFrame`; the per-frame updates live in plain helper functions and the camera comes from the frame callback
- [x] Found while building it: the bottle's rise ran frame by frame, so SwiftShader (slow, as in CI and screenshots) caught it half-risen; it runs on the clock now. `capture-showcase` launches Chromium with SwiftShader and waits for `load` on Klin (a second visit could sit waiting for network idle)
- [x] Feedback 2026-11-01: the hero and the Monsoon chapter looked the same. The hero is now a storm (the bottle floats and bobs, lightning flashes the sky every few seconds, banks of mist drift, a cone of light with turning dust falls on it); Monsoon stands the bottle on wet stone (Poly Haven CC0, credited) over a blurred mirror, with rain rings spreading on it
- [x] Feedback 2026-11-01: Agarwood reads as wood: a dark wood floor (Poly Haven CC0) that mirrors the bottle, incense smoke rising in soft wisps, embers that bloom. Jasmine stands on black lacquer under its petals, and the collection lines up on the wood
- [x] Feedback 2026-11-01: choosing a size in the pop-up reshapes the drawn bottle (30 ml slim and short, 50 ml the house flacon, 100 ml tall and broad), morphing outline, juice level, collar and cap with a spring; every size shares the same points so the outline can morph
- [x] The 3D bottle takes the chosen size too: on wider screens the scent sheet opens on the left like a configurator over a light veil (no blur), and the bottle of that scent (the main bottle in a chapter, its own place in the line-up) eases to the slim 30 ml or broad 100 ml proportions, growing from its base; phones keep the drawn bottle that morphs
- [x] Found while fixing CI: the engraving asked `document.fonts.load()` for the whole `--font-bodoni` stack, which includes next/font's local() fallback face; Linux (CI) has no Times New Roman, so the load rejected with "A network error occurred" and failed the page's console check. The load is now caught and falls back to whatever face is ready
- [x] Found while fixing CI: on phones, a top-centre toast covered the sticky header, so Talad's "Basket" button couldn't be tapped while "added to basket" showed (and stayed, paused under the pointer). Toasts now open below the header everywhere (`--toast-top`, taller for Kaffa's shipping strip and Talad's phone search row); e2e checks the toast sits below the header
- [x] Feedback 2026-11-01, first luxury pass: bloom, fine grain and vignette (postprocessing), refracting glass with colour fringing (drei MeshTransmissionMaterial), "KLIN / BANGKOK" engraved in gold on the glass (drawn in the page's Bodoni once the font loads), dust turning in the beam, a slow camera drift. The full look switches on only once a big screen holds a good frame rate (drei PerformanceMonitor) and drops back if it sags; phones and slow machines keep the light scene
- [x] Found while building it: headless Chromium draws WebGL on the CPU (SwiftShader, about 2.5 fps for this page), and CI failed the Klin e2e on GitHub's 2-CPU runners even with the first, lighter scene.
